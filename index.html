How to Play

Shepherd the Sky

Catch falling Stars, Wishes, and Moonbeams on your cloud to bounce them upward into the unlit constellation nodes.

Chain Combos

Keep bouncing celestial light without letting stars slip past to climb up to an x8 score multiplier. Complete every constellation to advance waves.

Survive the Storm

Dodge Lightning telegraph beams and Storm Clouds. Shatter Heavy Meteors by bouncing rising stars into them or using a Cloud Gust (Space / Click) before they hit you or the village!

Celestial Power-Ups

Catch glowing orbs for Wide Cloud, Slow Time, and Starlight Shield. Survive the Tempest Sovereign boss every 5th wave!
